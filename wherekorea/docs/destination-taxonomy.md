# Destination Taxonomy — WhereKorea v1

## Naming rule
Use simple, traveler-recognizable names for main cards.
Put richer sub-structure inside detail pages.

## Core v1 destinations

### Seoul
Role:
Primary urban gateway and comparison anchor.

### Busan
Role:
Large coastal city with strong city + sea contrast versus Seoul.

### Jeju
Role:
Signature leisure and island destination.
Notes:
Detail page may include Hallasan as a natural layer.

### Gyeongju
Role:
History-heavy destination with a calm, heritage-forward identity.

### Jeonju
Role:
Food + hanok + short-stay cultural destination.

### Gangneung
Role:
Accessible east-coast café-and-beach destination.
Tag grouping:
East Coast

### Sokcho
Role:
East-coast gateway with market + sea + mountain access.
Notes:
Detail page includes Seoraksan context.
Tag grouping:
East Coast

### Tongyeong
Role:
Harbor, islands, slower southern coastal character.

### Namhae
Role:
Nature-forward, scenic, quieter southern destination.

### Jirisan
Role:
Mountain and hiking-led destination node.
Notes:
Detail page can explain trail access and approach bases, but main card name stays simple.

## Expanded v1.1 candidates
- Andong
- Incheon

## Groupings for filtering or collections
- East Coast: Gangneung, Sokcho
- History / heritage: Gyeongju, Jeonju, Andong
- Mountain / hiking: Jirisan, Sokcho
- Southern coast: Tongyeong, Namhae, Busan
