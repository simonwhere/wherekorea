# Editorial Guidelines — WhereKorea

## Tone
- practical
- grounded
- traveler-helpful
- clear over clever

## Use these patterns
- best for
- skip if
- easier with a car
- works well as a 2-day trip
- crowded on peak weekends

## Avoid these patterns
- tourism-board exaggeration
- dramatic superlatives without evidence
- vague shorthand such as “reuse the WhereNow asset”

## Required editorial fields per destination
- one-line vibe
- best for
- skip if
- recommended stay
- food character
- insider tips

## Hook rule
A hook should help a traveler decide, not just admire the place.

Bad:
- A magical coastal jewel with endless beauty

Better:
- Best for a slow southern coast trip with scenery, seafood, and a quieter pace than Busan
